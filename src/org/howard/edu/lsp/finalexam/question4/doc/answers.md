**Name:** Pierre Merry

At the start of this semester I thought of AI mostly as a shortcut. My perspective has changed — I now see it as a thinking partner rather than a replacement for understanding. Working through assignments like the ETL pipeline and IntegerSet, AI was most useful when I used it to clarify concepts rather than just generate code. The times I copied output without understanding it I ran into problems I could not debug.

Going forward I plan to use AI for conceptual questions and design decisions, but I will always make sure I can explain and defend whatever ends up in my submissions. The most valuable skill is knowing how to ask the right questions and critically evaluating responses rather than accepting them at face value.
